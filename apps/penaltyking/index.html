About this app

Take your national team all the way to the World Cup title — one penalty at a time!

Summer 2026. The whole nation is holding its breath. But this time it's not 90 minutes — it's one kick, one shootout. Tap one of five corners of the goal to aim, then stop the power-timing bar in the green zone for a rocket shot! Miss it, and the next keeper will laugh in your face. The simplest, most thrilling penalty kick soccer game out there.

A long, brutal World Cup road from the group stage to the final

  • Group stage starts with Czechia → Mexico → South Africa
  • Punch through the Round of 32 (Japan), Round of 16 (Brazil), Quarterfinals (Germany), and Semis (France) — one goal at a time
  • The final boss? A goalkeeper from outer space. Beat them to win the World Cup!
