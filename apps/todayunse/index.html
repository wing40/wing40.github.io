About this app

See today's luck on a single card every morning, and pick good days for the month on a calendar.

Instead of scores, you get a clear mark such as "● Good day" or "▲ Caution" plus one line explaining why.

Daily luck by birthday and Chinese zodiac

Add your birthday and today is read from the day you were born. Add only your sign and we use your Chinese zodiac.

One short line tells you why, for example "Today pairs with your birthday".

Today's lucky color and one small thing to do are on the same card.

Lucky days calendar

Every date this month carries a mark: ● good, ▲ caution, ◇ slight, ⌂ spirit-free day.

Tap "Moving" to keep only days that are both good and spirit-free, a handy shortlist for moving dates.

"Interview · Trip" keeps only good days, and "Family, no ▲" keeps days when nobody in the family needs to be careful.

Not sure what a spirit-free day is? Tap the question mark and the explanation opens right there.

The whole family on one screen

Add yourself and up to 8 family members, and the home screen shows one line such as "Our home today: 1 caution".

Tap a name to open that person's card.

2027 New Year fortune

Your year by Chinese zodiac on one card. 2027 is the Year of the Goat; see how it meets your sign.

Morning reminder and widget

One line for your home arrives every morning at 8. No ads in notifications.

The 2×1 home-screen widget turns to the new day at midnight.

Share cards

Send today's card or your family card to a group chat as a neat image.

Good to know

Marks follow traditional zodiac rules (harmony and clash) and are for fun and reference. Please make important decisions yourself.

All features are free with no purchases; the app is supported by a bottom banner and full-screen ads.

No login. What you enter stays on your phone.
