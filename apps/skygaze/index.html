About this app

Skygaze is the simple way to never miss what's happening in the night sky.

🌠 COUNTDOWN to every upcoming sky event — meteor showers, solar & lunar eclipses, supermoons.

📸 REAL PREVIEW photos so you know exactly what each event looks like — even if you can't get outside to see it.

📍 FROM YOUR LOCATION — we compute your local best viewing time, the direction to look, and how much the Moon will get in the way.

🔔 ONE GENTLE REMINDER each morning at 9am about what's coming up. No spam.

Honest by design: if an event isn't visible from your area, we say so — and point you to where it is. Times and directions are computed on your device (no account, works offline).

Coming up in 2026: the Perseid meteor shower on a moonless night (the best since 2018), a total solar eclipse over Europe, and a deep "blood moon" eclipse.

Skygaze is not affiliated with NASA, ESA or any space agency. Images are public-domain / Creative Commons, credited in-app.
