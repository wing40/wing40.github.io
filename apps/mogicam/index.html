About this app

Turn an idle phone into a mosquito-detection camera.

Ever flipped the lights on at night, hunting one mosquito along the wall?

Park a spare phone facing a spot where mosquitoes tend to land — a white ceiling

or a bathroom wall — and Mogicam watches the view. When it sees a mosquito-like

spot, it alerts you with a photo.

Mogicam does not repel or kill mosquitoes. It's a camera that finds and tells you

where one landed. Going over to deal with it is up to you.

  • How to use
  • Prop a charging phone where you often see mosquitoes (a white ceiling or wall).
  • Aim it at a bright, plain surface about 1–1.5 m away (brighter is better).
  • Start watching and go do your thing. You'll get a sound/vibration alert on a find.
  • Features

· Real-time video analysis detects resting or moving spots (processed on-device only)

· Sound & vibration alert on a find + a red circle marking the spot

· Optional auto screenshot — check later whether it really was a mosquito

· Setup guide, charging reminder, dim-screen mode for night

  • What it needs to work well (told honestly)

· It needs a close mount (~1–1.5 m) and some light. A pitch-dark room is too dark.

· It works best on plain, single-color backgrounds (white ceiling/plain wall).

Moldings, edges and busy patterns increase false positives.

· It does not guarantee catching every mosquito — some may be missed.

  • Privacy

· Camera frames are analyzed on your device only and never sent anywhere.

· Screenshots are saved only when you turn them on, and only to your device gallery.
