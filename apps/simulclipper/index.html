About this app

Switch it on, hold it close, and watch them jump. Your phone buzzes like a real hair clipper.

The sound changes when you touch someone

This is not just a sound board. Hold the phone near a head or neck and the motor bogs down, the tone drops, and the vibration gets heavier, exactly like a real clipper biting into hair. Pull away and it spins back up.

Four guard lengths

3mm, 6mm, 9mm and 12mm. A longer guard makes the motor work harder and the sound gets deeper. The difference is clear enough to hear.

Sound and vibration move together

The motor spins up when you switch it on and winds down with inertia when you switch it off. The vibration follows the sound instead of running on its own. You can turn vibration off with a switch.

Records

The app keeps your total run time, how long you actually spent cutting, and how many times you switched it on. You can share a card with your numbers.

Try this

Behind someone dozing on the couch. Near a friend's neck when they are not paying attention. A short buzz is much funnier than a long one.

Please note

This app only produces sound and vibration. No hair is actually cut. It is meant for fun only. Do not use it on people who startle easily, on small children, or on pets.

Internet permission is used only to show ads. The app itself works without a connection. There is a banner at the bottom, and no full screen ad interrupts you while you are using the clipper.
