About this app

In Korean families, each generation traditionally shares one hanja syllable in their names — the "dolimja" (generation name). Many clans choose these characters so their radicals cycle through the Five Elements: Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water.

Dolimja Calculator is a simple offline reference tool for this tradition.

Check your generation names

Add your family's dolimja characters in order (grandfather → father → you) and instantly see whether they follow the Five Elements cycle, with the reasoning shown — which radical belongs to which element, and why the result came out that way.

Next-generation candidates

Based on your last generation's character, browse hanja candidates that fit the next element in the cycle. Handy when naming a new baby.

Element dictionary

Commonly used generation-name characters grouped by element, with meaning and reading. Useful for a character you have never seen in the family record.

Fully offline

No account, no server. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

Note: results are a reference based on the common Five Elements naming convention. Each clan's official genealogy may differ — please confirm with your family elders before actual naming.
