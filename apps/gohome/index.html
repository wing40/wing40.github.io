About this app

It's 6 PM. Mr. Kim just wants to go home. But the office won't let him.

Go-Home King is NOT a game where you just press a button and leave.

Your boss's "Hey, got a sec?", the surprise team dinner at 6:59 PM, the client call right as you stand up — survive every soul-crushing moment of office life and cover the 1000m to home.

  • Hold RUN to move forward! Let go to "look busy" (safe)
  • Get caught running in a boss's gaze (yellow band) → an event explodes
  • Run out of Stamina/Mental or miss the last bus → you sleep at the office
  • 120+ absurd office events (more coming)
  • Negotiate with your boss, build your Nunchi gauge, items, achievements

A game where failing is funnier than winning. For everyone who failed to clock out on time today.
