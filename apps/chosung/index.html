Chosung Quiz - Korean Words

Guess Korean words from initial consonants. 480 puzzles plus a host mode.

Get it on Google Play — free

About this app

  • *ㅊㅅㄱㅅ — can you read it? It spells the name of this game.**

Korean words can be written as just their initial consonants, and native speakers play a guessing game with them. This app turns that game into 480 puzzles across eight topics: food, animals, four-character idioms, places, jobs, objects, nature and weather, and body and mind.

  • *Play solo**

Pick a topic and take on ten puzzles. You get the consonants and a one-line hint, then fill each syllable slot. Every slot offers candidate syllables that **share the same initial consonant** as the answer, so the consonants alone never give it away. Tap a filled slot to clear it.

Stuck? Three hints are available for coins: reveal a letter, cut down the choices, or show the answer. After each round you get a score, a rank, and a review list of everything you missed, with the answer and its explanation.

  • *Present mode — hosting a quiz**

A screen made for the person running the game at a party, a club, or a classroom. Choose a topic and how many questions, and each puzzle fills the screen in large type. Tap to reveal the answer, tap again for the next one. No timer, no scoreboard, nothing but the puzzle. Turn the phone sideways and it grows for a bigger display.

  • *No ads appear at all while Present mode is running** — it is a screen you show to a room. Questions never repeat inside a set, and recent ones are avoided in the next set.
  • *Word collection**

Every word you get right becomes a card. Words you have not met yet show only their consonants, which is usually enough to make you play one more round. Coins keep accruing slowly while the app is closed.

This is a fully offline game. All words and hints were written from common nouns and traditional idioms; no real person or trademark is used. There are no in-app purchases — hint coins are earned for free in-game. Ads are included to keep the app free.
