About this app

🧩 Daily Word Sort — one puzzle a day to wake up your brain! A word association puzzle, Korean-style.

You get 16 scrambled words. Sort them into 4 groups of 4 that share something in common. Sounds easy? Traps are hiding everywhere. You only get 4 lives! 😈

🇰🇷 Tricky by design

Built around Korean homophones and Chinese-character double meanings — but the brain-bending fun translates. Every puzzle hides a twist that makes you second-guess the obvious answer.

👆 Easy to learn

Your first launch starts a hands-on practice puzzle with 8 words. It checks your taps, points out mistakes right away, and explains why the trap word is a trap.

💥 Clear feedback on every miss

Wrong answers show red tiles, a shake, a "Wrong!" stamp, a breaking heart, and a buzz of vibration. If you are one word away, it tells you that too.

⏰ Daily reminder at 9:40 AM

A notification shows today's puzzle number and a hint of today's trap word. You can turn it off in settings.

😈 Villain of the Day

The sneakiest trap word becomes a character! A new villain tests your brain every single day.

🏆 Fun result ranks

Sorting King · Know-It-All · Wrong-Track Master · Hopeless Today… what's your brain rank today?

Brag to friends with a spoiler-free color-block share card — results only, no answers leaked.

📊 National success rate & stats

"Was it just me, or was this one brutal?" Check the just-for-fun nationwide success rate and your own records.

🔥 Streaks

Play one puzzle a day and build your streak.

📚 100 puzzles + endless Practice mode
