About this app

Your past life was… a peasant. 🪖

Just drop in one photo. Past Life Test reveals which past-life rank you really (?) were — from Joseon-era servants and palace eunuchs to maharajas, pharaohs, Inca emperors and Aztec priests. One of 134 ranks across 29 eras worldwide is your true past life.

  • Features

· One photo → instantly reveals one past-life rank (134 ranks worldwide)

· 5 rarity tiers: from "common" to "legendary past life"

· Fake "match rate" and an "appraiser's verdict" make it feel real

· The same photo always gives the same result — compare with friends

· 👥 Group ranking: add 2–6 photos and reveal who outranks whom

· 🤝 Two-photo bond: what were you two in a past life? (Master & servant? Cellmates?)

· 🐾 Pet mode: 11 exclusive past lives for your dog or cat

· Collect every card you pull, earn era-completion badges

· Save & share as a meme image

  • How it works

We read the brightness pattern of your photo to pick a single number that decides your past life. That's why the same photo always gives the same result! It's not fortune-telling, not face recognition, not AI physiognomy — just a gacha made for laughs.

※ This app is 100% entertainment. It has no scientific, fortune-telling, or psychological-diagnosis basis, and all results are auto-generated jokes. Your photo is only briefly read on-device to derive a number seed — it is never stored or sent anywhere.
