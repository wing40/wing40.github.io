About this app

The wax ball you were too afraid to crack — here you get infinite ones.

Swapping to a new ball never costs you an ad. No mess to throw away, nothing to worry about touching.

That crunch, in your fingertips

Tap once and the spot cracks; tap again and the piece breaks off and tumbles down. Drag your finger and the shell crumbles along the path. Crisp crunching sound plus haptic feedback makes every hit land.

Rarer shells are made of different stuff

It is not just a prettier color, the whole feel changes. Chocolate snaps off in big chunks. Glass shatters wide in a single hit. Metal takes three hits on the same spot but pays double coins, and crystal breaks into tiny shards so it lasts far longer. Every material has its own sound and vibration.

Cracking tools: pick, roller, hammer

Spend your coins on a tool and keep it forever. The pick punches narrow and deep, the roller sweeps a wide path as you drag, and the hammer lands one huge blow. The same ball feels different depending on what you hold. Tools only change the feel, never your rewards.

A new ball every day

A fresh ball lands every midnight, and taking it never costs an ad. Shells you have not collected yet come first, and every day you show up adds a stamp worth more coins than the last. Skip a day and you keep every stamp you already earned.

Your record

Balls cracked, cracked today, pieces broken off, days in a row, and a count for every material. Finish a ball and you can share a card with the before and the after side by side.

Squish the ball inside

Once the shell is fully peeled, the squishy ball is yours. Press it and it squashes, let go and it bounces back. No goal, no timer — poke it as long as you like.

Roll for new balls

Every ball you finish earns coins, and coins buy new rolls. Salt bread, cookie dough, butter bars, macarons, mint choco, matcha, caramel, star candy — you never know which shell hides which core until you crack it. Once in a while a legendary one shows up with its own effect.

22 shells × 20 cores, a 42-slot collection

Every new shell and core is logged automatically. Locked slots show as silhouettes, and unlocked ones can be tapped to bring that ball back. Fill 30 slots and the workshop opens, letting you pick any shell and any core you own and build the exact ball you want.

A limited shell, this week only

A new limited shell rotates in every Monday, and you can claim one for free each week. Some feels, like bubble wrap, turn up only here. The collection shows how many weeks until each one comes back around.

Every sound in the app is made from scratch, and everything runs fully offline with no internet connection needed. No sign-up, no personal data collected.
