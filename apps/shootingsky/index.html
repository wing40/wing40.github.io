About this app

A classic vertical scrolling jet shoot 'em up — "Sky Raid"

That old arcade feeling is back, in a light, easy-to-pick-up vertical shooter. Fly a fictional fighter with one thumb, weaving between drones, enemy squadrons, flak cannons, and bombers across five airspaces. There's no fire button — your guns fire automatically, so you can focus purely on dodging and shooting.

One-thumb drag controls — a vertical shooter you start in one second

  • Your jet follows your finger as much as you drag (relative feel, no teleport)
  • Auto-fire is always on — no aiming stress, just pure dodging
  • A tiny hitbox (a small point at the center of your jet) — squeeze through barrages by a hair

Five stages that keep getting tougher

  • Training airspace (day) → coastal air defense → night infiltration → over the fleet (heavy rain) → the enemy main force
  • Drones, light enemy fighters, ground flak, enemy fighters, bombers, and supply transports — new enemy mixes each stage
  • Indestructible hazards like thunderclouds and turbulence must simply be avoided

★ A mid-boss AND a giant stage boss in every stage

  • A mid-boss appears alone midway through each stage — beat it for a guaranteed weapon power-up or bomb drop (miss it and you get nothing!)
  • A giant boss ends each stage — a recon airship, an assault helicarrier, a stealth bomber, an Aegis destroyer, an enemy carrier — each tougher than the last
  • Multi-phase boss fights that shift patterns at 66% and 33% HP. Later bosses require destroying wing guns and turrets before the core

Collect and power up

  • Weapon power-ups from Lv1 to Lv5 — more simultaneous fire streams mean more firepower
  • Toggle red (straight) / blue (fan) fire to match the situation
  • Bombs — wipe enemy bullets off the screen and deal big boss damage (start with 2, bank up to 3)
  • 3 lives + generous invincible respawn — keep most of your firepower even after dying

3 difficulty tiers, beginner-friendly

  • Easy · Normal · Hard — start on Easy, or crank it up to Hard for faster, denser barrages
  • Your best score is saved on your device — beat yesterday's you

Great for

  • Anyone who misses old-school arcade vertical shooters and bullet-hell games
  • Fans of short, punchy pixel retro arcade fun
  • Offline play supported — fully offline, no sign-up, no server
