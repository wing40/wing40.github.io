About this app

How fast are you, really?

The moment red turns green — how many milliseconds does it take you to react? Reflex Battle is a collection of 5 quick tests for your reflexes and short-term memory. Each round takes seconds, and you'll want "just one more" every time.

⚡ Reaction Time — tap the instant it turns green. A battle measured in milliseconds.

🔢 Number Memory — memorize where the numbers are, then tap them in order.

🎵 Sequence — repeat a flashing pattern that grows longer each round.

👆 Tap Speed — how many taps can you land in 5 seconds?

⏱ Time Sense — stop at exactly 5.00 seconds, with the timer hidden.

See your percentile instantly

When a test ends, you get your score, a "Top X%" rank, and a fun grade — from Superhuman to Half-asleep.

Summon a friend with a challenge card

Turn your result into a shareable image card and dare a friend: "Can you beat this?" They take the challenge, and the rivalry begins.

Runs fully offline with no server, and collects no personal data beyond advertising.

  • Just for fun — not a medical or scientific measurement, and the "Top %" figures are approximate.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who loves quick reflex and brain mini-games
  • Friends and family who want a reaction-speed showdown
  • Score-chasers who go "one more time" in spare moments
