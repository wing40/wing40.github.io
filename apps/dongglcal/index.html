Stamp each day on a habit calendar and see your whole month on a home widget

About this app

One goal is enough. Circle Calendar Widget keeps only what matters — a single goal line, a stamp on each day, and a home-screen widget you see every day.

How it works

  • Write your one goal at the top. (e.g. "Work out 30 min", "Drink 2L of water")
  • Tap a day to stamp it — a circle (○) if you kept it, a triangle (△) if so-so, an ✕ if you missed it.
  • Tap again to change the stamp. Just like the "well done" stamps from grade school.

The home-screen widget is the heart of this app

  • Put the widget on your home screen and see this month's goal and stamps at a glance.
  • You stay aware of how consistent you've been — without even opening the app.
  • After midnight the widget rolls over to today on its own (the part other apps often get wrong).
  • Tap the widget to open the app and stamp right away.

A gentle daily reminder, too

  • Set your own time and message, and get a nudge every day. (e.g. 10 PM "Did you keep your goal today?")

10 calendar skins for every mood

  • From a minimal developer-style grid to urban monochrome, sweet pink, dark neon, cream pastel, sage green, ocean blue, sunset peach, retro pixel, and mocha brown.
  • Switch a skin and the whole calendar, widget, and stamp colors change with it.

Everything is stored only on your device. No sign-up, no personal info. Completely free, completely offline.
