About this app

Turn your phone into a patrol car light bar.

Tap once and the siren starts, the on-screen light bar spins, the camera flash fires, and the phone buzzes in your hand — all locked to the same beat. Right as the siren hits its highest note, the light sweeps past the front.

🚨 5 sirens, built live — no recordings

Every sound is synthesized the instant it plays, not a sound file: Wail (the slow sweep), Yelp (the fast sweep), Phaser (for junctions), Hi-Lo (the European two-tone), and Air Horn (the fire-engine horn).

💡 The camera flash fires too

No extra permission needed. Turn the lights off and point it at the ceiling — the whole room lights up on beat.

📖 Siren guide

Never could tell the sirens apart? The guide explains what each of the 5 sounds is and where you would actually hear it, plus why you shouldn't use lights and sirens like these on a real vehicle.

📊 Records

Tracks how many times you've switched it on, your total run time, and your favorite siren — shareable as a card.

  • A toy siren for fun. Not a real emergency signal, and not connected to any police or fire service. Keep it indoors, among people who know it's a joke.

Runs fully offline with no server.
