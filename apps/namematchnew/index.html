About this app

Just type in two names! Dugeun is a name love test entertainment app based on Korean character strokes. Watch as each letter breaks down into strokes and the numbers add up into a love match percentage through a cute animation. Try a name compatibility test with a friend, a crush, your partner, or anyone for a fun heart-fluttering moment.

  • What makes it fun
  • Love match % — calculates compatibility from Korean name strokes
  • 24-tier results — rich result comments that change with every score
  • 3 tone modes — replay the same match in Normal / Stylish / Silly vibes
  • Calculation animation — see letters turn into strokes and merge into a tree
  • Share card — Instagram-story-ready images to show off your result
  • 100% offline & free — no sign-up, everything free with ads
  • Your names stay private

Names you enter are used only for on-device calculation. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. No sign-up, no personal data collected.

  • Switch tones, double the fun

Even with the same two people, changing the tone of the result comment gives a whole new feel. Go Stylish when you want it serious, or Silly when you want a laugh with friends! You'll never get bored running the couple match test again and again.

Dugeun is a just-for-fun entertainment app — not fortune-telling, divination, or any kind of diagnosis. Kick back and enjoy the flutter of a name love test!
