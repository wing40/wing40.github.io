About this app

"You're putting THAT in the bibimbap?" Gather your friends, each bring one secret ingredient, and mix it all into one big bowl — the Random Bibimbap challenge, now easy to set up with one phone. Perfect for office lunches, school clubs, family gatherings, retreats, and workshops.

How to play

  • Pick how many people (2–10)
  • Pick a mode
  • Safe mode: each person is secretly assigned a *category* (rice, sauce, protein, veggie, topping…). No missing essentials, no all-kimchi disaster — but the exact item stays *your* secret.
  • Chaos mode: assign random specific ingredients. Overlaps and gaps included. Brace for despair mode!
  • Pass the phone around — each person peeks at their own assignment only
  • Grab your item, meet up, and mix it all together

Open the lids · Tally

  • Tap the ingredients that actually showed up and get judged: Hope route / Just fine / Despair route
  • Crown today's Hero ingredient as the MVP (sesame oil, bulgogi, fried egg…)
  • Save and share your result card

Ingredient Codex

  • Browse the Hall of Fame and 57 ingredients across five tiers

The real point of random bibimbap isn't the food — it's being together. "Who brought this?" sparks a conversation, and everyone's one side dish adds up to a whole meal.

Everything runs on your device. No sign-up, no personal data, completely free and fully offline.
