About this app

One phone is all you need. The chaos is on you.

You know that awkward 5-minute silence at the bar, the dorm trip, the house party, or date night when nobody knows what to do? This app exists to kill that. No sign-up, no internet. Just open it and pass the phone around.

▼ Three games (each one its own kind of unhinged)

🔄 Pass the Phone

A call-out mission pops up like "Hand this phone to whoever's the worst at holding their drink." You all glance around, point fingers, and shove the phone at someone. When the round ends, a tell-all stats card drops, who's tonight's life of the party, who's tonight's pushover, screenshot it and drop it in the group chat forever.

🎯 Finger Roulette

Everyone puts a finger on the screen. The roulette spins and picks one loser. That person does the dare. Real multitouch, so everyone has to actually join in. The fastest, fairest luck-of-the-draw there is.

⚡ Reaction Duel

When the screen flashes green, whoever taps first wins. Slowest tapper takes the dare. A few drinks in and everyone's fingers get clumsy, which only makes it funnier.
