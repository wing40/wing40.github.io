About this app

Tojeong Bigyeol, the traditional Korean New Year fortune, with the math shown

Enter only your birth date. No birth time, no gender. Tap the three slots one by one (upper, middle, lower) and each slot shows a readable line of calculation, for example your Korean age plus the year number, divided by 8. At the end you get a three-digit gwae number. Solar dates are converted to the lunar calendar for you.

Yearly and monthly readings

Your gwae opens two lines of classical Chinese handed down since the late Joseon period, with the Korean reading and a modern interpretation. All twelve monthly readings are open with no lock, and the current month is highlighted. You can share a monthly reading as an image.

The whole family at a glance

Add up to 8 people, such as parents, partner and children, and see everyone's New Year fortune on a single screen with the Whole Family sheet. Gather around one phone at the Lunar New Year, or send the share image to your family chat.

Monthly reminder

On the first day of each lunar month you get one notification that the month's reading is open. Turn it off any time in your phone's app settings.

What to know

  • No payments and no login. Everything is open, including the monthly readings.
  • Your birth dates and family list stay on your phone.
  • Ads are one bottom banner and one interstitial when you draw. There are no ads in the middle of a reading.
  • The app is in Korean and English.
