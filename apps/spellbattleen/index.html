About this app

their / there, your / you're, its / it's, affect / effect…

Even native speakers mix these up. How confident are you?

Spelling Battle is a fun, two-choice quiz on commonly confused English words, played by filling in the blank in real sentences. No rote memorizing — just pick the word that fits.

  • How to play
  • Confusing words like their/there and your/you're appear as sentence blanks
  • Answer 10 questions, then see your score and rank
  • Every answer comes with a "why it's correct" explanation (in English, plus Korean)
  • Take a daily challenge and revisit your mistakes in Review mode
  • Turn your result into a "challenge card" and dare your friends
  • Great for
  • Anyone interested in English vocabulary and everyday spelling
  • Learners who want a light check on confusing English words and grammar
  • Friends who want to battle it out and see who knows English best
  • Features
  • 178 questions covering common spelling mix-ups and confusable words
  • Fully offline — play anywhere, no internet needed
  • No login, no account — light and clean
  • Scores and history stay on your device only

※ Spelling Battle is a casual quiz app made purely for entertainment.
