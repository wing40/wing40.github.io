About this app

🧥🕶️ Silver hair, dark shades, leather jacket — the CEO is here to catch GPUs!

A dead-simple, ridiculously addictive catch game! "Jensen," the CEO of an AI chip company,

slides left and right across the bottom of the screen and catches falling chips and graphics

cards — with his head. One finger, drag left and right, that's it. Anyone can pick it up in

30 seconds, and the higher your score climbs, the harder it is to put down.

  • How to play
  • Drag Jensen left and right to catch the falling items with his head.
  • Every catch pumps up your "Market Cap (in trillions)" — that's your score!
  • Catch items in a row to build a COMBO and watch your score snowball.
  • You only get 3 lives. Get hit by a bomb and you lose a life — and the device buzzes.
  • Catch these
  • 🎮 Gaming GPU (GTX 9090) — beloved by gamers
  • 🧠 CPU chip (G200) — brains at full throttle
  • 🟨 Gold graphics card (Gold Edition) — a shining score bomb
  • 🚀 AI Factory (RedStar) — the factory that prints the future
  • Avoid these
  • 💣 Tariff Bomb — lose a life and feel the buzz
  • 📉 Black Monday — market crash; lose a life
  • Features
  • Free to play (contains ads)
  • No internet, no login required — your high score is saved safely on your device
  • Korean and English supported, portrait mode
  • Lightweight and fast
  • Rumor has it there's a hidden invincibility cheat... (hint: tap the game-over skull 10 times!)

📈 How high can your market cap go? Start moving Jensen right now!
